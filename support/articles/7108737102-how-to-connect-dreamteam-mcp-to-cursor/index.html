Connecting Cursor to Dreamteam CRM

Last updated 7 days ago

You're deep in a Cursor session and need to check a deal or pull a contact - without breaking flow.

Dreamteam MCP lets Cursor talk to your CRM directly. Ask Cursor to look up a contact, update a deal, or pull pipeline data - it goes through your Dreamteam workspace, respects your permissions, and returns the answer right in your chat. Setup takes a minute.

Before you start

You'll need:

  • An active Dreamteam CRM account

  • Cursor installed (any recent version)

Everyone uses the same address, whatever your company is called:

https://mcp.dreamteamcrm.ai/mcp

Dreamteam works out which workspace to open from the email address you sign in with, so there is nothing to look up before you start.

Adding the MCP server

  1. Open Cursor and click the + button on the homepage.

  2. Choose MCP, then click Manage.

  3. Click Add Custom MCP and paste your Dreamteam MCP URL.

  4. Hit Save, then click Authenticate.

Your browser opens a Dreamteam page headed Connect your AI assistant. Sign in with your email address, or with Google or Microsoft if that is how you normally sign in to Dreamteam. If your email address belongs to more than one Dreamteam workspace, Dreamteam asks you to Choose an organization next - pick the one you want Cursor to work in. Approve the access request and you are returned to Cursor.

Once you're back, Dreamteam is connected and all 17 tools are enabled automatically.

What you can do next

Once connected, Cursor can look up, filter, create, update, and delete records across contacts, companies, deals, notes, and any custom objects you've set up.

For the full list of tools, permissions guidance, and example prompts, see the Dreamteam MCP documentation.

Good to know

  • All 17 Dreamteam tools enable automatically on connection - no per-tool toggling required.

  • Your CRM permissions apply - if you can't see a record in the app, you can't see it through MCP either.

  • Disconnect anytime - remove the connection from Cursor's MCP settings.

If Cursor shows the server as connected but no tools appear, write to us at gethelp@dreamteam.ai.
