Common pricing questions

What counts as a seat?

A sales rep or manager connected to your Klozdesk workspace and WhatsApp-as-the-UI approval bot. Unaccepted invites are never billed.

How do AI credit packs work?

Credits power the work your AI teammates do: AI enrichment, the Ask Klozdesk assistant and your four AI coworkers - Zara, Koda, Tobi and Oga. A pack is ₦10,000 per month and is shared by your whole team. Your credits expire at the end of each billing cycle.

How many packs do we need?

Each ₦10,000 credit pack covers 500 AI WhatsApp/IG conversations and 60 minutes of voice note & call intelligence. Top up anytime via Paystack.

Are the AI teammates priced separately?

No. Zara, Koda, Tobi, and Oga all draw from the same shared workspace credit wallet. Official WhatsApp Cloud API and webhook sync are included.

Who’s a viewer?

Anyone who only needs to see the pipeline. Viewer seats are free.

Is there a free trial, and do we need a card?

Yes! You can test Klozdesk immediately in our 10-Minute WhatsApp Sandbox by uploading your brochure PDF, or choose our Done-For-You White-Glove setup by Sparkr Digitals.
