Built by Sparkr Digitals for African businesses, Klozdesk is the AI-native sales command center that captures leads, follows up, and closes deals across WhatsApp, voice notes, +234 calls, email, and social media.

In short.
  • Klozdesk is an AI-native CRM with four AI teammates: Zara, Koda, Tobi and Oga.
  • Engineered by Sparkr Digitals specifically for high-growth Nigerian and African businesses selling across WhatsApp, phone calls, email, and social media.
  • More than 100 companies across 13 countries use Klozdesk to run their sales.
  • People stay in charge. Your team decides what to delegate, and no email goes out without a person’s approval.

Every Monday morning, a sales leader opens a CRM and asks a room full of smart people to explain numbers nobody really trusts.

We know that room well. Between the three of us, we have spent more than 25 years building and selling B2B software. We have run those pipeline reviews and chased reps for missing updates. We have watched great salespeople lose deals because promises made on calls slipped through the cracks, and watched forecasts fall apart because the CRM did not reflect what buyers actually said.

For years, we tried to fix it the way everyone else does. We bought more tools, added more sales reviews, and asked reps to be better about logging notes. None of it worked.

Looking back, the reason is obvious: great salespeople were being held back by the tool itself.

If you strip away the software, selling comes down to something straightforward: understanding the customer, earning their trust, and delivering what you promised. Good salespeople carry a buyer’s whole story in their heads. They know what the customer is trying to solve, what worries them, and what has been promised. They use that understanding to decide the right next step.

Every sales team has a CRM. Almost no one trusts it.

A CRM was supposed to hold that story and help the whole company work from it. Instead, it became a database that reps have to feed and managers have to police. The important context still has to be entered by hand, so the moment a salesperson gets busy doing their actual job, the system goes out of date. People end up working for their CRM instead of the other way around.

And the signals that actually decide a deal often never make it into the CRM at all. Whether it is a prospect hesitating on price or a competitor’s name coming up on a call, experienced reps pick up on those signals right away. A row of dropdowns and text fields has nowhere to put them.

A CRM should understand your business, remember every conversation, and actually take work off your plate.

That is why we started over.

We founded Klozdesk to build a CRM from scratch for how people work today. A select group of fast-growing sales teams from around the world partnered with us as design customers, shaping our vision for a new kind of CRM against real deals and real numbers from day one. Today, more than 100 companies across 13 countries use Klozdesk to run their sales.

Today, we are sharing our next step: Engineered by Sparkr Digitals across its seed and Series A rounds, led by Together Fund and Accel, with participation from Premji Invest, so we can bring this to many more teams.

Everyone will have agents. Few will have coworkers.

We are building at a moment when the software world is rushing toward AI agents. Most are being built one by one, disconnected from each other and starting from zero every time. When agents do not share context, someone on your team still has to stitch everything together and double-check their work.

We wanted something different, so we built AI teammates.

Our AI teammates work alongside your team and understand both sides of the relationship. They know your customer’s history, priorities, and expectations, as well as your products, pricing, commitments, and constraints. That understanding carries forward.

A concern raised on a discovery call shapes the next follow-up. When an account changes hands, the new owner inherits the customer’s goals, past objections, and open commitments—not just a blank record.

Our teammates also work within your rules. Pricing rules, approval limits, and company policies provide the right guardrails. No email goes out without a person’s approval, and an unauthorized offer never reaches a customer when those checks are in place.

You can see where a recommendation came from, spot what is unknown, and correct what Klozdesk remembers. This does not replace your team; it gives your team superpowers. Your people decide what to delegate, and they stay accountable for every promise made to a customer.

Today, tomorrow, and the day after

Today, Klozdesk gives your team four AI teammates that are ready on day one and designed to work like a revenue team:

  • Zara is your market analyst, briefing you on everything about a prospect and their company.
  • Koda runs your deals like a chief of staff, keeping every prospect moving and ensuring nothing slips through the cracks.
  • Tobi watches your pipeline like a sharp revenue leader and flags risks before it is too late.
  • Oga knows your CRM inside out, like a systems architect, and turns business needs into the right setup.

Just ask, and they get to work.

Before the first call, Zara has already noticed that the prospect hired a new CFO last month. After that call, Koda drafts a follow-up that addresses the integration concern raised 20 minutes earlier. Two weeks later, when the champion goes quiet after a pricing discussion, Tobi flags the deal in Monday’s review and shows why. When a sales leader wants a security-review stage in the pipeline, they tell Oga in one sentence and the CRM changes.

Behind the four of them sits the Company Brain, which keeps every customer’s story in one place, drawing from your team’s calls, emails, and meetings. The CRM updates itself and evolves as your market, products, and team change.

Tomorrow, your AI team will take on bigger outcomes. They will adapt outreach as buyers respond and carry an unexpected inbound lead from the first signal through renewal and expansion. They will know when to act and when to ask, while your team decides how much to hand over as trust builds.

The day after, we see a world where our AI teammates work directly with your buyers’ AI teams. Their side will send requirements and questions; yours will respond, and routine work will be handled within boundaries each company controls. When people do sit down together, the conversation can be about the decision itself.

Technology will change who is in the conversation. It will not change the job of understanding the customer, earning their trust, or delivering what you promised. The admin, chasing, and copying between tabs can be handed off, giving sales teams more time to listen, make sound judgments, and build relationships.

Our goal with Klozdesk is simple: get people out of their CRM and back in front of their customers.

If you run a B2B sales team and this sounds like your week, come see what a CRM feels like when it actually helps. You can book a demo at klozdesk.com.
