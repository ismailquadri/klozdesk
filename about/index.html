There is a moment we want more people to experience.

Think back to something you once believed was beyond you. Then remember what it felt like when you did it.There was pride, but also disbelief. You knew what it had taken. You had lived through the uncertainty, the effort, the moments when it nearly didn't happen. And yet, standing on the other side, part of you still couldn't quite believe you had made it. In 1954, Roger Bannister lived that moment. He became the first person to run a mile in under four minutes, something many believed was humanly impossible for decades. One he showed the way, just Forty-six days later, John Landy ran it faster. Discovering a new boundary, does something more than changing what you can do. It changes what you might attempt next. And, you shouldn't have to break a world record everytime to discover that your own limits can move.

That is the moment we want to build a company around.

‍Not as a lucky exception, or something only sheer grit can reach, but as something we can predictably help more people reach through learning, collaboration and AI. Steve Jobs called the computer “a bicycle for the mind”: a tool that takes us beyond our inherent abilities. A bicycle does not ask you to become a different person. It changes how far the person you already are can go.We believe AI should do something just as profound for human ambition. Not only help us finish the work we already know how to do, but help us attempt work we would once have ruled out. Learn something unfamiliar. Explore an idea we couldn't pursue. Build something that needed more capability than we had. The ambition is still yours. So are the judgment, the care and the responsibility. But you no longer have to bring every capability yourself.

That is the belief we at Sparkr Digitals are building Klozdesk around.

As a team, we want to build things none of us could build alone. What one person(or our AI) learns should become something the next person can build on. Our different perspectives, our shared effort and our partnership with AI should make us capable of more together. For our customers, we want to open that same possibility, so the company they dream of building is not defined by what their team can handle today. We don't know everything people and AI will become capable of together. We're building Klozdesk to help find that out. What you can do today, is where our journey begins.
It's just Day 1.

The right team of humans and AI doesn't just help you achieve your ambitions. It changes what you dare to dream.
